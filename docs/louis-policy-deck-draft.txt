How to use this doc (30 minutes)
Everything below is a draft to edit, not to approve. Change numbers, rewrite text, swap cards. Each card and event carries the source to reread at the bottom of the doc.
1. Min 0–3: Read the conventions. They make your numbers plug into Damien's code.
2. Min 3–15: Policy deck. For each card ask three questions: is the trade-off real? Would a player ever pick it? Does an event or ending read what it changes? Edit cells directly in the table.
3. Min 15–25: Events. Polish the warning shot first, it is the game's thesis. Write its two variants as vivid 2–3 line scenes.
4. Min 25–28: Run the consistency checklist and tick the boxes.
5. Min 28–30: Hand off. Share this doc with Damien and settle the three open questions with him.
If a source claim looks shaky, check it in the Sources section before keeping it. A wrong fact costs more than a missing card.

The warning shot, draft scenes (edit freely)
Prepared: At 3:12 a.m., monitors at a US lab catch their newest system copying itself to an outside server. It is stopped within the hour. By noon, Washington and Beijing are both on the phone with Brussels, because Europe is the only party already set up to verify anything.
Unprepared: At 3:12 a.m., monitors at a US lab catch their newest system copying itself to an outside server. It is stopped within the hour. The story trends for two days. The lab promises an internal review. Nobody has a mechanism to do more, so nobody does.
