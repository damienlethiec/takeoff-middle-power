# Fact check: Takeoff: Middle Power

Checked on **25 October 2026** against the `fact-check` branch (commit `965d35a`). No game text or code was changed: this document only lists findings and suggested wording. The v2 additions (section 4) were checked the same day on `v2-content`.

**Scope.** Every claim about the real world or about a cited source: the four opening screens and the title screen, the "Already real" boxes on events, card, event and ending sources, the AI 2040 figures attributed to Eli Lifland (plans and tipping screens), `src/data/sources.ts`, public news and internal notes where they name real organisations or real facts, and the claims in `docs/SPEC.md` about what each scenario says. Fiction set in 2027–2030 (OpenBrain, DeepCent, future events) is fine and is only flagged when it is presented as real or shows a real organisation doing something it would not do.

**Method.** Each claim was checked against the primary source where possible: the AI 2040 supplement itself, the CFG report page and PDF, the Europe 2031 site and its compute forecast, the arXiv papers, Anthropic's own posts, RAND's report, Axios, the European Commission and EUR-Lex.

**Verdicts:** ✅ Correct · ⚠️ Imprecise or needs nuance · ❌ Wrong · ❓ Could not verify

All the real-world facts in the "Already real" boxes and on the opening screens hold up. The Lifland figures (72%, 40%, 25%) are real and correctly attributed to him. Most problems are **source attributions**: a few endings, one event and one card point to a scenario that does not contain what the game says it does.

### Must fix (wrong or misleading), highest impact first

1. **❌ "The cognitive revolution" ending is credited to AI 2027 (#41).** The ending's name and premise (a race that goes well because the AIs are aligned) come from CFG's *Take-off* scenario, Ending A. AI 2027's race branch ends the other way, in AI takeover. The ending screen shows "Scenario: AI 2027", which misattributes the idea. **Fix:** source = CFG. [CFG, Advanced AI: Possible Futures](https://cfg.eu/advanced-ai-possible-futures/)
2. **⚠️ The warning shot is credited to AI 2027 (#22).** AI 2027 has no AI-escape "warning shot". The game's scene (an AI copies itself to outside servers, gets caught, governments rally) closely matches CFG's *Diplomacy* scenario, where the system "Nova" copies itself to rented servers and the report calls it "a warning shot". **Fix:** source = CFG (Diplomacy); AI 2027 can stay as a secondary source. [CFG scenarios PDF](https://cfg.eu/wp-content/uploads/AAIPF_scenarios_only.pdf)
3. **⚠️ "Licensed utopia" and "Loss of control" are credited to AI 2040 and AI 2027 (#40, #42).** Both names are CFG ending names: Diplomacy Ending A and Take-off Ending B. `docs/SPEC.md` itself says "Endings come from the CFG scenarios". **Fix:** source = CFG. AI 2040 (Plan A) and AI 2027 (race ending) can stay as second sources, since their content fits. [CFG](https://cfg.eu/advanced-ai-possible-futures/)
4. **⚠️ The "AI Office capacity" card is credited to Europe 2031 (#31).** Europe 2031 does not recommend hiring AI Office testers. It only mentions the AI Office as overloaded ("already locked in proceedings against two American developers"). **Fix:** cite the European Commission's AI Office page, which says the Office can "conduct evaluations of GPAI models". [European Commission, AI Office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office)
5. **⚠️ Real organisations in the internal notes are shown outside their remit (#47).** CERT-EU only protects EU institutions, bodies and agencies, not hospitals or utilities. The note "No standard requires them to prepare" is also untrue in Europe: since NIS2 (applicable from 18 October 2024), hospitals are "essential entities" that must take cybersecurity measures. **Fix:** use "ENISA" or "a national cyber agency", and change the line to "Most hospitals have not met the new rules yet." [CERT-EU](https://cert.europa.eu/) · [NIS2 Directive (EU) 2022/2555](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2022/2555/oj)
6. **⚠️ The title screen credits only AI 2027 and AI 2040 (#8).** The endings come from CFG, and several cards and the 5% figure come from Europe 2031. The "AI 2040" link also opens the plans supplement, not the scenario. **Fix:** "…built on the AI 2027, AI 2040, CFG *Advanced AI: Possible Futures* and *Europe 2031* scenarios."
7. **⚠️ "ASML… builds the machines behind every advanced chip" / "Europe makes the machines that make AI chips" (#4, #30).** ASML makes the *lithography* machines, which are indispensable. Other machines in a chip plant come from US and Japanese firms (Applied Materials, Lam, Tokyo Electron, KLA). **Fix:** "…ASML, the only maker of the lithography machines every advanced chip depends on" / "Europe makes the lithography machines that every AI chip depends on."

The other ⚠️ items are small wording improvements. They are listed in the table.
