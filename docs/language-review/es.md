# Spanish review (es)

Native-level review of `src/i18n/es.json` against the reviewed English (`src/i18n/en.json`), 27 Oct 2026. Neutral
European Spanish that reads naturally in Latin America; English is the reference for meaning, the Spanish is written
to sound written in Spanish.

## Register

- The interface, the tips and the cards address the player as **tú**; the friends write with **tú** (and **vosotros**
  when they speak to "you lot" in Brussels). No gender is assumed for the player: Léa opens "¡Hola, cuánto tiempo!"
  rather than "querido amigo / querida amiga"; adjectives about the player are avoided.
- Léa: oral, warm, concrete ("qué nivel", "meter la pata", "Cansada, nada más"); signs "L.".
- The mother: "Cariño," … "Un beso, mamá"; practical, nurse's words (historia clínica, planta, triaje, turnos).
- Malik: "¡Soy Malik!" while enthusiastic, shorter and quieter as the race closes in; signs "M.". "Back home" is
  rendered "en Europa" (never "en casa", which reads as his flat).
- Pan-Hispanic words preferred to Spain-only slang where the two diverge: regañar (not echar la bronca), detectar (not
  pillar), simpáticos (not monos).
